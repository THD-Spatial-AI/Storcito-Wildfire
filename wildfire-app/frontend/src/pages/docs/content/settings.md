# Settings, profile & feedback

Open **Settings** from your profile menu (your initial at the bottom of the sidebar).

## Settings

| Tab | What you can change |
|---|---|
| **General** | Interface **language**, default **weather location** and saved **map locations** |
| **Display** | Units for temperature, distance and wind speed, the data refresh interval, **text size** (small, normal, large) and the model-creation intro card |
| **Notifications** | Email when a calculation completes, browser notifications and system alerts |
| **Privacy & Terms** | Review or change your Data & Privacy consent for the map |

Saved map locations sync to your account, so they follow you to other devices.

## Profile

**Profile** shows your name, email and access level. You can update your personal details and change your password here. Your access level is set by an administrator.

## Notifications

The bell in the top bar shows new notifications, for example when a calculation completes or fails, or when maintenance is scheduled. Click a notification to jump to the related model. Use **Mark all read** or **Clear all** to tidy the list. The full history is on the **Notifications** page.

Notifications arrive live while the app is open. If you enabled email notifications, you also get an email when a model completes.

## Feedback

Choose **?** (Help) → **Feedback** to send a message to the team.

1. Pick a category: **Bug Report**, **Feature Request**, **Improvement** or **General**.
2. Add a subject and a message. The message supports Markdown, and you can preview it before sending.
3. Optionally attach a screenshot and give a rating.

Your earlier submissions and their status are listed under **My feedback**. Closed feedback is removed automatically after 7 days.

When reporting a bug, include what you did, what you expected, what happened instead, and the app version shown at the bottom of the sidebar.
