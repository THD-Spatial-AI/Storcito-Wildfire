# Comparing models

**Model comparison** puts two completed models side by side. It is available to managers and experts from the sidebar, or by selecting exactly two completed models in Wildfire Simulations and choosing **Compare**.

## Choosing models

1. Pick a **Baseline** model, the reference.
2. Pick a **Comparison** model, the one you want to measure against the baseline.

## Reading the comparison

**Summary.** A headline tells you whether risk *increased*, was *reduced* or is *broadly stable*, followed by:

- **High + Very High share** in each model,
- the **dominant class** of each model,
- the **analyzed area**, which can differ because only cells with valid data are counted.

**How to read the direction:** more High/Very High means the comparison model is more severe. More Very Low/Low usually means risk shifted toward safer classes.

**Charts**

| Chart | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Distribution comparison** | Share of area per risk class in each model |
| **Risk flow** | How much area moved from each baseline class (left) to each comparison class (right) |
| **Biggest changes** | The largest class-to-class shifts, with how much stayed the same, rose or fell |
| **Risk score distribution** | Frequency of the continuous score. A curve further right is more severe |
| **Area by risk class** | Absolute area per class, comparable even when model areas differ in size |

The cell-by-cell change analysis needs both models to cover the same area and grid. If they don't, use the side-by-side maps and the distribution charts instead.
